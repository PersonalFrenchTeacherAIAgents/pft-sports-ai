# Personal French Teacher Sports AI

Communication-focused AI learning environments for professional and international sport.

## Sports collection

**Football · Tennis · Golf · Grand Racing**

## What Sports AI does

Sports AI focuses on professional communication around high-level sport: interviews and media, French-speaking coaches and staff, sponsors and public messaging, technical vocabulary, international professional environments and repeated communication scenarios.

## What Sports AI does not do

These systems are not athletic coaching tools. They do not teach football technique, tennis technique, golf swings or racing driving. Their role is **professional communication and language learning** within each sport.

## Why continuous availability matters

Athletes work around training, travel, competition, recovery and media obligations. A permanently available agent reduces scheduling friction and lets the learner rehearse a scenario immediately before a professional situation.
